Selected work

Building dubizzle Oman from zero awareness

Regional Marketing Manager, dubizzle Group. Real estate and cars.

The challenge

dubizzle entered Oman with a presence under the OLX Oman name, so the dubizzle name meant nothing to the market. The main competitor, OpenSooq, had around 20 years in the country. The brand had to start from nothing and win against an entrenched incumbent.

The goal

Localize a brand that came from the UAE and the wider GCC, build awareness and traffic, and make the business profitable and revenue-generating.

A brand that sounds Omani

All communication was in local Arabic, with Omani figures in every campaign, and the brand showed up where locals already were. I created a local persona for the brand, with his own personality and way of talking. It was a new, non-visual way of expressing the brand that dubizzle Group had not used before.

One asset to own on the street

A $1.5M out-of-home campaign ran in two bursts. The idea was simple: recognition rises when a brand is tied to one specific element. We tied dubizzle to cars and dominated the streets.

Buzz on purpose

I brought together Oman’s major influencers in a single campaign and positioned it as the most expensive marketing campaign in the country. The aim was for people to talk about the spend, and in doing so, learn the name.

A jingle that became a trend

A catchy, local brand jingle came with a social challenge: post a video with the track and enter a prize draw. Three major influencers sang and danced to it until it trended.

Always-on performance and community

Digital campaigns drove leads and traffic, supported by banners across the website. Social coverage of car showrooms, local real estate projects, and major national events turned our accounts into an information hub.
